Privacy policy

KidHome and KidHome Listen, made by Oddvalue. Effective 16 October 2026.

In short: the apps don't collect, share or sell any information about you or your child. Neither app has permission to use the internet, so nothing you or your child do in them leaves the device. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

What KidHome keeps on the device

  • The home screen you build: page names, which apps are on it, tile colours, labels and emoji, and your child's first name if you type one in for the greeting.
  • Your parent PIN, stored only as a salted hash. The PIN itself is never saved.
  • Whether locked folders have been unlocked today, and whether the app guard is switched on.

App guard

If you turn on the app guard, KidHome uses Android's usage access to check which app is on screen while the screen is on, so it can bring your child back to KidHome and ask for your PIN. It reads this on the device as it happens and keeps no record of which apps were used.

Google Family Link

KidHome reads which apps Android has hidden or paused, which is how it follows the limits you set in Family Link. It can open the Family Link app or website when you tap those buttons. It doesn't read your Family Link account, settings or activity. KidHome isn't made by or affiliated with Google.

What KidHome Listen keeps on the device

  • Listen reads the music and audiobook files stored on the device so it can show and play them. It never uploads, changes or deletes them.
  • It saves where each audiobook was left, and your last sleep timer choice.
  • It creates the sleep sounds (rain, ocean and so on) on the device.

Children

Both apps are made for children to use after a parent or carer sets them up. Children can't send us any information through the apps, because the apps have no way to send anything.

Removing your data

Everything the apps keep stays on the device. Uninstalling an app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes it. Your music and audiobook files aren't touched.
